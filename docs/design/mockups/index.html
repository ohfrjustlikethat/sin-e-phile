Three takes on one brief

Same content, same palette, same constraints. They differ in typography and layout — which is what is being chosen between.

Every still is a real frame pulled with FFmpeg from a public-domain film on the Internet Archive. Real titles, real credits, real synopses. No placeholders, per the brief — grey boxes hide exactly the problems worth seeing.

Take A

Editorial

The most MUBI-led. Bets on emptiness: one enormous still, very little else, and a serif that only appears at title sizes. Fewest titles above the fold, most atmosphere.

All three share the revised §9.1 palette: warm near-black greys, non-linear value steps, oxblood reserved for intent. All three obey §9.0 — no purple, no glass, no gradients as decoration, no emoji, no bento grids, depth from surface value and 1px lines only, at most two radius values, and deliberately irregular card sizes. All three use the ADR-0020 solid opaque player panel, and all three show the ADR-0013 typographic card for the film with no artwork.

Fonts are self-hosted woff2 in ./fonts/ — no network requests, per §9.2.
