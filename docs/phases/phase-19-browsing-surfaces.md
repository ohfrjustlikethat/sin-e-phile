# Phase 19 — Browsing Surfaces

**Status:** in_progress · **Depends on:** 18 · **Sessions:** 4–5

## Goal

Home, Films, and TV Shows become the beautiful, navigable front of the whole system, and the whole app is brought to one design standard before the phase closes.

## Deliverables

**Idle in TV mode** (ADR-0043): after 5 minutes, an ambient screen (a slow slideshow of artwork, or typographic title cards without artwork, with the clock); any button returns where the viewer was. **No launch intro** (ADR-0043): the first painted frame is Home. **Leaving the player** (ADR-0044): the picture shrinks (about 350 ms, a critically damped spring) into the tile it was launched from while the page fades in behind it; with no tile on screen it shrinks to the centre and dissolves; reduced motion makes it a 200 ms dissolve. **Shared-element motion** (ADR-0044): a poster grows into its film page and the page's artwork into the player, inside `src/design-system/focus/`. **Seamless resume** (ADR-0044): relaunching restores the page, its scroll and focus, and offers the film at its position; after a crash the same state returns and the crash is said plainly, once. **Home**: Continue Watching first (with progress, next episode, and a remove option), then adaptive rails generated by the discovery engine, ordered by predicted engagement, with an optional hero for a single strong recommendation. **Films** and **TV Shows**: Continue Watching scoped to that kind, then browsable structure — by genre, decade, country, language, director, movement, collection, awards, and curated lists — with real filtering and sorting. **Every rail has a "see all"** that opens a proper browsable grid with filters, honouring the anti-pattern rule that nothing is more than three interactions from Home. Detail pages: hero artwork, synopsis, cast and crew with links, ratings, availability badges (local / streamable / downloadable, with quality and language), episode lists with progress for series, related and "more like this", and a prominent play button with the language and quality selectors from Phase 9. Hover previews: muted, 400 ms dwell, still-frame-then-motion, disableable. A Live Channels tab with an honest, well-designed empty state pointing to Phase 25. **The TV billboard** (ADR-0042): the top of Home is a typographic billboard for the focused or featured film — title in the display face, year, director, one line of why — and behind it **the focused film's full artwork backdrop** when the user has a TMDB key, cross-fading as focus moves; with no key, a dim tint (designed first, since it is the default). Everything virtualised, everything keyboard-navigable, everything at 60 fps.

## Exit criteria

- [ ] **E1** Home renders in < 800 ms on Tier 0 with 20 rails.
- [ ] **E2** Anything in the catalogue is reachable in ≤ 3 interactions from Home.
- [ ] **E3** Every rail's "see all" goes somewhere genuinely useful.
- [ ] **E4** Full keyboard navigation across all browsing surfaces, including the masthead's tab order: wordmark, destinations, Search, Downloads, Settings, then content.
- [ ] **E5** The masthead fits at the 1024×640 minimum window with every destination, Search, Downloads and Settings visible, measured by `tools/uiaudit`.
- [ ] **E6** Search is reachable from every surface by the visible Search button and by `/`.
- [ ] **E7** No destination or state shows a placeholder screen.
- [ ] **E8** Every screen is usable with an Xbox controller alone, search included through an on-screen keyboard built for the D-pad (ADR-0041), in TV mode with the focus language of ADR-0042.
- [ ] **E9** Home's billboard shows the focused film's artwork backdrop with a TMDB key and the designed tint without one, cross-fading in under 300 ms as focus moves, at 60 fps.
- [ ] **E10** The design lint passes in CI, and each of its rules has been seen to fail.
- [ ] **E11** Leaving the player shrinks the picture into its tile at 60 fps, measured in the real window, and reduced motion replaces it with a dissolve (ADR-0044).
- [ ] **E12** Relaunching, including after a forced crash, returns to the same page, scroll, focus and film position (ADR-0044).
- [ ] **E13** The author signs off the screenshot review set (every surface, both sizes, with and without artwork) against §9.0's test: `manual: <what they reviewed and said>`. This set supplies the README's screenshots.
